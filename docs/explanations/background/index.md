else: what a Pulseq file says and what it leaves implicit, what the PulSeg
representation adds to it, how Nimpulseq and Nimpulseqgui approach the same
problems, and what MRD carries back to the reconstruction.

These four pages are a recap of published work, kept deliberately free of
Pulserver specifics — where Pulserver takes a different route, the
{doc}`sequence model <../sequence_model/index>` pages say so and why.
